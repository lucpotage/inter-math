Inter Math, OpenType

The OpenType math font, its nine weights (src/mathml.css) or variable (src/mathml-variable.css): the same LaTeX as MathML (KaTeX's MathML output), laid out by the browser from the font's MATH table, beside KaTeX's own layout in Inter Math. The variable font's rules and gaps follow the weight only where the browser applies the MATH table's variations (HarfBuzz after 14.6.0): until then they stay Regular's. The OpenType font's em is Inter's (its math as large as Inter's text): here its letters are set as large as KaTeX sets Inter Math's, so that the two compare. MathJax 4 sets the same LaTeX with the font made of it for MathJax (src/mathjax-font/, build/mathjax.py), at its one weight, Regular.
